# Tally Ho: Halloween party poster

A warm, slightly mischievous costume-party invitation. Guests should remember the
big orange lettering and the ghost who came dressed for the occasion. This theme
is event presentation; Halloween does not change eligibility or the data model.

## Typography
- Creepster: hero title and brand only. Its irregular horror-poster shapes make
  the invitation memorable without making controls harder to read.
- Barlow Condensed 600/700: section titles, entry titles, and ticket labels.
- Nunito Sans 400/600/700: forms, ballots, feedback, and body text.
- Fonts are bundled locally through Fontsource, with serif/sans-serif fallbacks.

## Colors
```css
--ink: #241b32;
--paper: #fff1d6;
--surface: #fff9ec;
--pumpkin: #ef6c25;
--purple: #65427b;
--muted: #68596d;
```
Use ink on paper for reading. Orange is a decoration and action background with
dark text. Purple with cream text marks selected ballots and filters. Thick ink
borders and offset shadows recall printed tickets and hand-pasted posters.

## Composition
The opening screen pairs a large, compact party-poster heading with an original
CSS ghost, pumpkin, moon, and cut-paper ticket. A dark status ribbon separates
the invitation from the useful gallery. Cards resemble entry cards pinned to a
party noticeboard. On phones the invitation stacks; ballots remain one column.

## Examples
- Do use Creepster for “Come as you aren't.” Keep “Clear choice” in Nunito Sans.
- Do keep entry numbers, individual/couple labels and no-photo placeholders clear.
- Do make selection visible with a radio, border, and color together.
- Don't add flickering, jump scares, autoplay, or moving decoration.
- Don't use spooky wording for errors that obscures what guests should do.
- Don't add TV/presentation views. Results belong on guest and host phones.
